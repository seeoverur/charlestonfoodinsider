A biscuit restaurant built for serious appetites

Charleston is famous for Southern food, and few dishes represent Southern comfort cooking better than a warm, freshly prepared biscuit. Vicious Biscuit takes that familiar foundation and turns it into an oversized breakfast experience filled with fried chicken, eggs, sausage gravy, pimento cheese, hot honey and other bold ingredients. The restaurant serves the Charleston area through its Mount Pleasant and Summerville locations, making it a convenient brunch stop for locals, beach visitors and travelers exploring the Lowcountry.

A Creative Approach to Southern Biscuits

The biscuit is the centerpiece of the Vicious Biscuit menu, but the restaurant does much more than serve a basic breakfast sandwich. Familiar Southern ingredients are combined in ways that emphasize sweet, spicy, savory and smoky flavors. These are not small sandwiches designed to be eaten in two bites. Most signature biscuits are complete comfort-food meals built around thick biscuits and generous portions.

One of the best-known selections is The Vicious, made with a cheddar-jalapeño biscuit, crispy fried chicken, maple sausage gravy, cowboy candy and maple syrup. The combination brings together sweetness, heat and richness in one plate. Diners who prefer pimento cheese can choose the Fat Boy, which pairs fried chicken with pimento cheese and hot honey on a buttermilk biscuit.

Guests who want more heat may be drawn to the Nashville Hot Chicken biscuit or the Flame Thrower. These menu items use hot sauce, peppers, bacon, cheese and house sauces to create a breakfast that is much bolder than a traditional bacon-and-egg sandwich.

Lowcountry Flavors on the Menu

Vicious Biscuit also incorporates dishes and ingredients associated with Charleston and the surrounding Lowcountry. Shrimp and grits are served with blackened shrimp, creamy Lowcountry gravy, stone-ground grits, white cheddar, bacon and an open-faced biscuit. The combination delivers a familiar Charleston-style dish while keeping the biscuit concept at the center of the meal.

The Crab Benny combines a house-made crab cake, fried green tomato, egg, hollandaise sauce and Old Bay seasoning over a biscuit. Another coastal choice is the Sea Biscuit, which pairs fried shrimp with sweet-and-spicy slaw, yum yum sauce, sesame and cilantro. These options make the restaurant a fitting stop for visitors who want recognizable coastal flavors in a casual brunch setting.

More Than Biscuit Sandwiches

Although biscuits are the main attraction, the menu includes several other breakfast and brunch options. The Bird and Belgian pairs fried chicken with a sweet-potato waffle, maple syrup and honey mustard. Diners looking for something more traditional can choose an Old School Breakfast with eggs, protein and a side.

Shareable starters help turn a simple breakfast into a larger brunch experience. Fried green tomatoes with pimento cheese, sweet-potato fritters and cream-cheese-filled beignets give families and groups several ways to sample the menu. Omelets, breakfast bowls, yogurt-based options and other plates offer alternatives for diners who may not want one of the restaurant’s largest biscuit creations.

A Casual Charleston-Area Brunch Experience

Vicious Biscuit follows a fast-casual service style, making it suitable for people who want a substantial breakfast without committing to a formal restaurant experience. Guests order before settling into the dining room, and the format helps the restaurant serve families, friend groups and weekend crowds more efficiently.

The Mount Pleasant location is especially convenient for travelers exploring Shem Creek, the Old Village, Sullivan’s Island or Isle of Palms. A heavy breakfast can become the first stop before a day of sightseeing, beach time or shopping. The Summerville location serves diners staying farther inland or traveling through the growing communities northwest of Charleston.

A Brunch Stop for Big Appetites

Vicious Biscuit is designed for diners who enjoy comfort food, generous portions and creative flavor combinations. Fried chicken, biscuits, gravy, cheese, bacon, hot honey and Southern-inspired sauces are central to the experience. This is not a restaurant centered on delicate breakfast plates or minimal ingredients.

Whether you order a straightforward sausage, egg and cheese biscuit or attempt one of the restaurant’s larger signature creations, Vicious Biscuit offers a memorable alternative to an ordinary breakfast. For visitors searching for brunch near Charleston, it provides a convenient way to experience bold Southern comfort food before exploring Mount Pleasant, Shem Creek or the nearby beaches. Arrive hungry and prepare for a biscuit that is anything but ordinary.
