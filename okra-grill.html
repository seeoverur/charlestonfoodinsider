Our Story

A neighborhood diner that grew up right

Page's Okra Grill opened its doors in 2006 and has been a fixture of Mount Pleasant breakfast and brunch ever since. It's family owned and run, and it still cooks like it — simple Southern plates, made from scratch, served without pretense.

Over the years the kitchen's reputation traveled well beyond Coleman Blvd: the shrimp & grits have been featured on the Travel Channel and in Southern Living, and the restaurant now has sister locations in Summerville and Moncks Corner. The Mount Pleasant original is still where it started.
