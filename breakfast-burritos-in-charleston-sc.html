One counter, one order. Start here.

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food opened on King Street as a coffee shop with a real kitchen behind it, and the burrito is the item that proves the point. It comes off the line hot, wrapped tight, and filled to the ends rather than stuffed in the middle and folded over empty tortilla.

What Goes Inside

The build is eggs, crispy potatoes and cheddar, with avocado and pico added for freshness and a green sauce served alongside. Steak is the protein on the house version. The potatoes are the detail that matters most: they stay crisp inside the wrap, which is where a lot of morning burritos go soft. The green sauce is bright enough to cut through the cheese, so use all of it.

If you would rather use a fork, the same ingredients are available as a bowl. The kitchen makes food in house and takes changes at the register, so swapping or adding a protein is a one-line request.

What to Drink With It

The simplest order is the combo, which pairs an egg and cheese burrito with an iced latte. From there the drink menu runs long. The Sweet Palm Latte is the house signature, made with vanilla bean, palm blossom and warm spices. The Palm Shaken Espresso is the stronger choice with oat milk and brown sugar. Non-coffee drinkers have ceremonial matcha, Carolina iced tea and a full list of dirty sodas.

When to Go

Breakfast is served all day, so this works as a late-morning or early-afternoon meal as well as a first stop. Weekday mornings are the easiest window. Saturday and Sunday between about 9:30 and 11:30 is when King Street fills up, and ordering ahead for pickup is the way around the line.

Where It Fits in a Charleston Day

The shop sits on the Upper King side of downtown, a short walk from Marion Square and the College of Charleston campus. That puts it on the route for a Saturday farmers market visit, a morning of shopping, or a walk south toward the historic district. Students use it as a between-class stop, which is also why it shows up in our CofC quick eats page. For the full picture of the cafe beyond this one item, read our Sweet Palm Coffee feature.
