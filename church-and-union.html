The Space

A century-old sanctuary, reimagined

Church and Union occupies a century-old church in the heart of downtown Charleston, with soaring 60-foot ceilings and original stained glass windows that cast color across the dining room through the day.

A 5th Street Group concept, the Charleston location opened in 2015 and pairs that dramatic architecture with a menu that leans playful and modern — a fitting stage for a kitchen unafraid to take a few creative risks.
